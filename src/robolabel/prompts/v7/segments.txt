Label the phases of one robot episode of {num_frames} frames ({duration} s at {fps} frames per second). The images
below are frames from it; the line before each image gives its frame number, time and camera.
The dataset's task description is: "{task}".

Objects in the scene (use these IDs):
{inventory_lines}

Gripper events measured by the robot itself:
{candidate_lines}
{attempt_lines}

Split the whole episode, from frame 0 to frame {last_frame}, into consecutive segments that do not overlap. Each
segment is one phase of what the robot does. phase_class is one of: approach, grasp, transport, release, retract,
press, pour, insert, fold, wipe, push, pull, rotate, open, close, other. phase_text is a short plain instruction that
names the object, for example "close on the red block".

Rules:
- The candidate frames above come from the robot's own gripper measurements and are exact. Keep them as segment
  boundaries unless the images clearly contradict them. A segment that ends at a candidate's boundary frame has
  boundary_source "signal" and that candidate_id; any other segment has boundary_source "vlm" and candidate_id "none".
- Add a boundary only where the images show an event the list lacks.
- There is no minimum number of segments.
- A retry is a new attempt with a higher attempt_idx, not a repeated phase. The phases of an attempt that failed get
  outcome "failed" and a failure_type (missed_grasp, slip, drop, wrong_object, misplace, press_no_effect, aborted,
  other); every other phase gets outcome "success" and failure_type "none".
- target is the ID of the object the phase acts on and destination the ID of where it is moved to. Use "unsure" if the
  images do not show which object, and "none" when there is no such object.
- Give at most 3 evidence items per segment: a frame and camera shown above and a short statement of what is visible.
- For every candidate in the list, give a verdict: confirm or reject, with a short note.
